The server reads the platform catalogue (`Open77.animations.list`) at start, so this list is what op77.123 ships: a later build may add rows, which are then offered without a release. The 15 rows marked ✎ are also written in `modules/animations/shared/catalogue.lua`; the written row wins (translated label, walking pace, curated variants).

- **Name**: what you type after `/e` or `/e with`.
- **Label**: what the picker shows (English locale).
- **Where**: what the clip was authored against. `chair`, `barstool`, `bed` and `ground` play where you stand, no furniture needed; `wall` and `counter` read best with one.
- **Prop**: the item the body holds.

```text
/e chair          play one
/e chair 2        play its variant 2
/e sitting        open the picker on a family
/e list           every offered name, with its variant count
```

A name in [`DISABLED`](/docs/opx_infinity/animations#config-animations-disabled) is not offered, alone or in a pair. `/e stop` is the stop command, so the `stop` gesture is only reached from the picker or as part of a pair.
