title: animations
description: Emotes for players — every platform animation, alone or with a nearby player, from /e, the F3 picker and the X stop key, plus walking paces and a client contract.
---

Players play emotes with `/e dance` or the picker on `F3` (built with the [menu](/docs/opx_infinity/menu)) and stop with `X`. Other modules use the contract to play an animation on the local player (a ripperdoc chair, drinking an item). The module plays nothing itself: solo emotes go to the platform's animation service, pairs to its two-player coordinator. It adds the picker, a per-player rate limit, a readiness check, the invitation and walking paces on the player's own eye menu. Nothing is written to the database.

| | |
|---|---|
| Side | both |
| Requires | none |
| Optional | `menu` (picker, invitation), `form` (picker search), `prompts` (stop key hint), `downed` (closes the picker), `target` (walking pace rows) |
| Contract | `animations` v1 — [client](/docs/opx_infinity/animations/client) |
| Config | `config/animations.lua` (shared) |
| Platform | `open77_animations` (solo, `players.animations.control`), `open77_player_interactions` (pairs, `players.interactions.control`) — both declared in the manifest |
| Data | none |

## Catalogue

Every profile the platform lists is offered: **104 on op77.123**, read at start with `Open77.animations.list`, so a profile a later build adds shows up at the next start. Fifteen rows are also written in `shared/catalogue.lua` and win on the same name. The full list is on [Catalogue](/docs/opx_infinity/animations/catalogue).

Not offered: names in [`DISABLED`](#config-animations-disabled), profiles whose `validation` word says they failed (`fail`, `broken`, `unsupported`…), malformed rows. The boot log has one line with the counts:

```text
[animations] platform catalogue: 104 profiles listed, 89 adopted (not adopted: written 15)
```

The picker draws 12 families, 16 emotes to a page. The platform's own family words map onto them:

| Family | Id (`/e <id>`) | Platform words |
|---|---|---|
| Gestures | `gestures` | `gestures` |
| On the move | `onthemove` | `onthemove` |
| Social | `social` | `social` |
| Emotions | `emotions` | `emotions` |
| Postures | `postures` | `postures` |
| Sitting | `sitting` | `seated` |
| Relaxation | `relaxation` | `relaxation` |
| Dance and music | `music` | `dance`, `music` |
| Consumables | `consumables` | `consumables` |
| Work | `work` | `work` |
| Interactions | `interactions` | `interactions` |
| More | `other` | any word this module does not know |

`/e dance` is the emote, not the family (hence `music` and `sitting`). A one-shot gesture (`wave`, `shrug`…) plays once for the clip length the platform measured; a `layer` profile (the `_walk` rows) keeps your own walking.

## With a nearby player [#duo]

Picker › **With a nearby player**, or `/e with …`. The server invites the **nearest** player within [`SHARED.RANGE`](#config-animations-shared), from positions it observes — no client names who is invited. Nothing plays until they accept.

| You pick | Typed | Coordinator kind |
|---|---|---|
| Carry them / Be carried | `/e with carry`, `/e with carried` | `carry` — fixed paired pose; the carrier walks, the other follows |
| Escort them / Be escorted | `/e with escort`, `/e with escorted` | `escort` — side by side, walking |
| Hand something over | `/e with give` | `give` |
| Look after them / Be looked after | `/e with heal`, `/e with healed` | `heal` — you kneel and examine, they sit wounded |
| A shortcut | `/e with talk`, `/e with film` | `custom` — a [`SHARED.PAIRS`](#config-animations-shared) row |
| Any two animations | `/e with <yours> [theirs]` | `custom` — one profile per body |

```text
/e with carry            invite the nearest player: you carry them
/e with dance            both dance
/e with preach armscrossed   you preach, they cross their arms
/e accept                the invited player says yes
/e decline               ... or no
```

- A paired move's id is read before a profile of the same word: `/e with carry` is the move, `/e with carry carry` is the `carry` profile on both.
- In the picker, *Any two animations* is two picks — yours, then theirs ("The same" on top) — so every ordered pair (104 × 104) is reachable without a 10 000-row list.
- The invited player gets a two-row menu (*Accept*, *Decline*) or a chat line naming `/e accept` and `/e decline`. **No name either way**: "The person next to you invites you: `{emote}`.", and the asker reads *sent*, *accepted* or *declined* without the other's name.

```text
ask ─► nearest player in RANGE invited ─► accept ─► distance measured again ─► pair starts
                                        ├► decline ─► asker told "declined" (no name)
                                        └► INVITE_MS passes ─► withdrawn, asker told "expired"
```

- An invitation counts against the same [`RATE_LIMIT`](#config-animations-rate-limit) window as a play.
- One invitation or pair per player, either side (`duo_busy`).
- The stop key, `/opx.anim.stop` or disconnecting ends the pair for both; the asker leaving withdraws the invitation.
- The coordinator also ends a pair on death, a vehicle, a bucket change, separation (`RANGE + 2` m, at most 20) or a failed presentation; both players are told why.

### What the platform cannot do [#limits]

- A `custom` pair is **stationary**: either body walking off ends it, a walking layer included. Only `carry` and `escort` move, and those two take no profile override (`paired_animation_fixed`).
- A pair plays a profile, not a clip: **no variant choice**.
- **Two players** per interaction, one interaction each.
- Solo emotes are refused by the service for a dead player or one in a vehicle (`player_not_alive`, `player_in_vehicle`).

## Commands

All four act on the caller only and are open by default.

| Command | Access | Arguments | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="opx-anim" />`/opx.anim` | everyone | `[name [variant] \| family \| stop \| list \| with <move\|name> [name] \| accept \| decline]` | None: opens the picker. A family: picker on it. A name: plays it. `with`: invites the nearest player ([above](#duo)). `accept`, `decline`: answers your invitation. |
| <a id="e" />`/e` | everyone | same as `/opx.anim` | Short form. |
| <a id="opx-anim-stop" />`/opx.anim.stop` | everyone | — | Stops your animation (and your pair), unless started as not cancelable. |
| <a id="opx-anim-list" />`/opx.anim.list` | everyone | — | Every offered animation by family, with variant counts. From the console: printed to the log. |

Names come from [`COMMANDS`](#config-animations-commands); `RESTRICTED = true` gates `command.<name>`.

## Configuration

`config/animations.lua` sets `OPX.Config.MODULES.animations`. Shared script.

| Key | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| <a id="config-animations-enabled" />`enabled` | `true` | `false` switches the module off. |
| <a id="config-animations-presenter" />`PRESENTER` | `'auto'` | Who poses the bodies on this client. `auto` leaves it to `open77_animations` while that runs; `always` / `never` force it. |
| <a id="config-animations-notify" />`NOTIFY` | `true` | Refusals as toasts; `false` writes chat lines. |
| <a id="config-animations-toast-ms" />`TOAST_MS` | `4000` | Toast duration, 750..120000. |
| <a id="config-animations-prompts" />`PROMPTS` | `true` | Stop key in the prompt strip while an animation plays. |
| <a id="config-animations-disabled" />`DISABLED` | `{}` | Names never offered, alone or in a pair: a written row, a platform profile (`'camera_point'`), or a paired move's id (`'escort'`, `'carried'`). A name matching nothing is a boot-log warning. |
| <a id="config-animations-platform" />`PLATFORM` | `true` | Offer every platform profile beside the written rows. `false`: the 15 written rows only. |
| <a id="config-animations-shared" />`SHARED` | see below | Emotes with a nearby player. Not a table, or `ENABLED = false`: none offered. |
| <a id="config-animations-loop-by-default" />`LOOP_BY_DEFAULT` | `true` | Loop when a request does not say. |
| <a id="config-animations-one-shot-ms" />`ONE_SHOT_MS` | `10000` | Length of a non-looping play with no duration (a one-shot platform gesture uses its measured clip). Tunable `ANIM_ONE_SHOT_MS`. |
| <a id="config-animations-max-duration-ms" />`MAX_DURATION_MS` | `600000` | Longest duration a request may name. Tunable `ANIM_MAX_DURATION_MS`. |
| <a id="config-animations-rate-limit" />`RATE_LIMIT` | `{ WINDOW_MS = 10000, REQUESTS = 6 }` | Plays and invitations per player per window; stops get twice as many. Checked before the request is validated. Tunables `ANIM_RATE_WINDOW_MS`, `ANIM_RATE_REQUESTS`. |
| <a id="config-animations-picker" />`PICKER` | `{ CLOSE_ON_SELECT = true, SHOW_VARIANT_WORDS = true }` | Close the picker after a choice; show the engine clip words beside each variant. |
| <a id="config-animations-keys" />`KEYS` | `{ PICKER = 'F3', STOP = 'X' }` | Default keys (`opx.animations.picker`, `opx.animations.stop`), rebindable in the pause menu. `false` registers none. The same key for both drops the stop key. |
| <a id="config-animations-walk-paces" />`WALK_PACES` | `stroll` 0.8, `walk` 1.2, `brisk` 1.8 | `{ ID, SPEED }` paces in m/s (0.5..2.5) a player cycles from their own eye menu. Past the last: normal movement. |
| <a id="config-animations-commands" />`COMMANDS` | `ANIM = opx.anim`, `EMOTE = e`, `STOP = opx.anim.stop`, `LIST = opx.anim.list`, all `RESTRICTED = false` | `{ KEY = { NAME, RESTRICTED } }`. `NAME = false` registers none. |

```lua title="config/animations.lua — SHARED"
SHARED = {
	ENABLED = true,
	RANGE = 3.0,         -- metres between the two bodies, 0.25..10
	INVITE_MS = 15000,   -- how long an invitation waits, 1000..60000
	KINDS = {            -- the platform's paired moves; DURATION_MS 500..600000
		carry  = { ENABLED = true, DURATION_MS = 60000 },
		escort = { ENABLED = true, DURATION_MS = 60000 },
		give   = { ENABLED = true, DURATION_MS = 5000 },
		heal   = { ENABLED = true, DURATION_MS = 12000 },
	},
	ANY = true,          -- any two profiles, one per body
	DURATION_MS = 30000, -- length of such a pair; two one-shot gestures use the longer clip
	PAIRS = {            -- shortcuts listed above "any two"
		{ ID = 'talk', ACTOR = 'preach', TARGET = 'armscrossed', DURATION_MS = 20000 },
		{ ID = 'film', ACTOR = 'record', TARGET = 'dance', DURATION_MS = 30000 },
	},
},
```

- `KINDS.<kind>.ENABLED = false` withholds both sides of that move. Only `carry`, `escort`, `give`, `heal` are kinds; another key is a boot warning.
- A `PAIRS` row: `ID` unique, lower case, not a move's id; its label is the locale key `animations.duo.name.<ID>` (without one the row reads its two profiles). A row naming a profile the build lacks or one in `DISABLED` is not offered.
- A bad value is a boot warning and falls back to the default shown.
- Pairs need `open77_player_interactions` running; without it the boot log says so and the picker has no *With a nearby player* row.

## Refusal codes [#codes]

A refusal caused by a contract call is not shown to the player (the caller decides); refusals from keys, commands and the picker are shown.

| Code | Meaning | Locale key |
|---|---|---|
| `unknown_animation` | Not in the catalogue, disabled, or not offered; for a pair, no such move, shortcut or profile. | `animations.error.unknownAnimation`, `animations.duo.unknown` |
| `unknown_category` | Not one of the twelve families. | — |
| `invalid_variant` | No such variant, or not offered. | `animations.error.invalidVariant` |
| `invalid_options` | Bad `options` field, or non-cancelable with no end. | `animations.error.invalidOptions` |
| `animation_locked` | A non-cancelable animation is running. | `animations.error.locked` |
| `rate_limited` | Over `RATE_LIMIT`. Only the first refusal per window is answered. | `animations.error.rateLimited` |
| `player_not_ready` | The player has not finished loading. | `animations.error.notReady` |
| `player_not_alive`, `player_in_vehicle`, `animation_owned` | Refused by the platform service. | `animations.error.notAlive`, `animations.error.inVehicle`, `animations.error.owned` |
| `service_unavailable` | No animation service, or `players.animations.control` not granted. | `animations.error.unavailable` |
| `play_raised`, `play_refused`, `stop_raised` | The service or coordinator call failed. | `animations.error.refused` |
| `not_sent`, `request_timeout` | The request did not leave, or got no answer in 15 s. | `animations.error.notSent`, `animations.error.timeout` |
| `no_player_nearby` | Nobody free and ready within `SHARED.RANGE`, in your bucket. | `animations.duo.nobody` |
| `duo_busy`, `player_reserved`, `animation_busy` | One of you already has an invitation or pair. | `animations.duo.busy` |
| `too_far`, `wrong_bucket`, `position_unavailable` | Apart when the invitation was accepted. | `animations.duo.tooFar` |
| `duo_unavailable`, `interactions_unavailable`, `paired_animation_fixed` | Pairs are off, or the coordinator is missing or refused. | `animations.duo.unavailable` |
| `menu_not_running`, `player_down`, `picker_not_open` | Picker only. | `animations.error.menuNotRunning` |
| `invalid_player`, `presentation_unavailable` | `State` only. | — |
| `internal_error` | A contract function raised. | — |
