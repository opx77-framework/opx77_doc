---
title: dealership
description: Vehicle dealers — buy a car from a marker, dress a showroom with locked preview cars, and let a salesperson sell to another player for a company account.
---

A player stands on a dealer marker and presses the key (**E** by default): a centred list shows the stock of that dealer's kind, grouped by class, then asks which of their garages to file the car under. The money is taken, the car is registered through [`vehicles`](/docs/opx_infinity/vehicles) and, by default, handed over on the marker. A dealer can also show locked **preview cars**, and inside a dealer's **zone** a salesperson can offer a car to another player, who accepts it on their own screen.

| | |
|---|---|
| Side | both |
| Requires | `character` (hard) |
| Optional | `vehicles`, `garages`, `prompts`, `menu`, `target`, `vehiclekeys` |
| Contract | `dealership` v1 — [server](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership/server), [client](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership/client) |
| Config | `config/dealership.lua` (shared) |
| Data | `opx77_company_accounts` (company balances), `opx77_company_pending` (deposits still owed); `opx77_dealerships`, `opx77_dealership_previews` (legacy, read-only) |

- Without `vehicles`: nothing can be sold.
- Without `garages`: every car is filed under the vehicles `DEFAULT_GARAGE`; no destination can be chosen.
- Without `menu`: no list, but `/opx.dealership.buy` still works.
- Without `target`: no "sell a vehicle" row.
- With [`vehiclekeys`](/docs/opx_infinity/vehiclekeys): the buyer receives a key with every sale (a full bag is said to the buyer and does not undo the sale).

## Commands

| Command | Access | Arguments | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="opx-dealership-list" />`/opx.dealership.list` | ACL `command.opx.dealership.list` | — | Every dealer (kind, label, position, heading, bucket, config or legacy) and every showroom car with whether it is standing. |
| <a id="opx-dealership-stock" />`/opx.dealership.stock` | everyone | — | Every stock row: kind, class, key, label, price. |
| <a id="opx-dealership-buy" />`/opx.dealership.buy` | everyone | `<key> [garage]` | Buys stock row `key` at the dealer the caller stands on, filed under `garage` if given. Same checks as the list. Cooldown `COOLDOWN_MS`. |

`command.opx.dealership.*` grants all three. Names come from [`COMMANDS`](#config-dealership-commands).

## How a sale works

1. The server re-checks everything: the dealer exists; the player is within `USE_RADIUS` in the same bucket; the dealer's kind sells that row (`garage` sells ground vehicles, `avpad` AVs); the currency is valid; the player can afford it. A chosen garage must be a garage of the same kind with a location in the player's bucket.
2. The price is taken with `character.RemoveMoney`.
3. The vehicle is registered with `vehicles.Register`. On failure (`vehicle.limit`, …) the price is refunded. If the refund also fails, the log says so and it must be settled by hand.
4. A key is cut (`vehiclekeys.Ensure`) when that module runs.
5. With `HAND_OVER = true` the car is created on the dealer marker, facing its `HEADING` (AVs `AV_LIFT` m higher). If the marker is taken, it tries one, then two car-widths to either side along the heading. All five taken: the sale still goes through and the buyer is told the car waits in its garage (`dealership.handOverBlocked`). A refused hand-over does not undo the sale: the car stays owned and filed.

- A refund (failed registration) and the seller's commission are paid to the **character**, not the connection: offline or switched, it goes through `AddMoneyOffline`.
- One purchase per citizen runs at a time (counter, `buy` command and accepted offer share one claim), so `PER_CHARACTER` cannot be passed by racing them.
- The nearest dealer is searched in the player's own bucket only.

### Selling to another player

- Inside `ZONE_RADIUS` of a dealer, the target eye shows **Sell them a vehicle** on every other player. The seller's list is titled *Sell to the person in front of you (#12)*; the buyer reads *You are being offered a `{model}`* and *Offered to you for `{price}`*. **No name either way**: the offer carries no seller, and the seller is never shown the buyer's account name. The buyer must answer within `OFFER_TIMEOUT_MS`; both must still be in the zone when they do.
- The counter's garage row reads *Buy, deliver here*. A hand-over refused for another reason than "every spot taken" says the car waits in the garage. A zero commission reads *Sale done: `{model}`. The whole price went to the company.*
- An offer that arrives while the buyer has their own dealership list open waits until that list closes. When an offer expires or the seller leaves, the buyer's offer screen closes ([`opx:net:dealership:withdrawn`](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership/events#opx-net-dealership-withdrawn)).
- At accept the seller's company is read again; a change refuses the sale with `dealership.sellerNoCompany`.
- On a yes the buyer picks one of their garages of that kind on their own screen ([`Decide`](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership/client#client-dealership-decide)), resolved in the buyer's bucket; none = the vehicles default garage; an unknown one is refused `dealership.noSuchGarage`. The buyer pays the full price and the sale runs the steps above.
- The seller's **company** receives the price minus the commission; the seller receives `SELLER_CUT_PERCENT` (rounded down). A commission that cannot be paid to the seller goes to the company.
- The company is the seller's job (when `COMPANY.JOBS` is on and the job is not in `EXCLUDED`), otherwise their gang (when `COMPANY.GANGS` is on). Neither: refused `dealership.noCompany`.
- Balances live in `opx77_company_accounts` (`kind` = `job` or `gang`, `group_key`, `balance`). Deposits only; no withdraw path. Read one with [`Balance`](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership/server#server-dealership-balance).
- A company deposit that fails is written to `opx77_company_pending` (`id`, `token` UNIQUE, `kind`, `group_key`, `amount`, `plate`, `created_at`) and the sale reports `pending = true`. A sweep (at boot, then every 60 s, 32 rows per pass) pays each row in and deletes it in one transaction, so a retry never pays twice. If even the ledger write fails, the deposit is held in memory and retried; a restart in that window loses it (logged as an error, settle by hand).

### Showroom previews

Each `PREVIEW.POINTS` row stands one stock model, **locked** and **persistent**, at most `PREVIEW.LIMIT` per dealer. A point naming a missing dealer is skipped with a log line. A point whose `ENTRY` is not a stock key is reported at boot.

### Legacy tables

- `/opx.dealership.add`, `/opx.dealership.remove` and the preview placement path (with its ACL right `opx.dealership.place`) **no longer exist**.
- At every boot the server reads `opx77_dealerships` and `opx77_dealership_previews`, adopts every row whose key is not in the config (config wins), and logs each as `[dealership] config line: ...`, ready to paste into `SPOTS` or `PREVIEW.POINTS`. Nothing writes to these tables any more.
- A role that still holds `opx.dealership.place` holds a right nothing checks.

## Configuration

`config/dealership.lua` sets `OPX.Config.MODULES.dealership`. Shared.

| Key | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| <a id="config-dealership-enabled" />`enabled` | `true` | `false` switches the module off. |
| <a id="config-dealership-currency" />`CURRENCY` | `'EDDIES'` | Money type prices are in. An unknown type refuses every sale (boot error). |
| <a id="config-dealership-hand-over" />`HAND_OVER` | `true` | Create the bought car on the dealer marker. `false` only files it. |
| <a id="config-dealership-use-radius" />`USE_RADIUS` | `4.0` | Flat metres from a dealer within which it can be used. The server allows [`REACH_SLACK`](/docs/opx_infinity/core/config#config-shared-reach-slack) (1 m) more. |
| <a id="config-dealership-marker" />`MARKER` | `garage` = cylinder/interaction/2.5, `avpad` = ring/interaction/3.5 | Marker look per kind: `{ shape, style, RADIUS }`. |
| <a id="config-dealership-max-distance" />`MAX_DISTANCE` | `150.0` | Metres beyond which a marker is not drawn (1–500). |
| <a id="config-dealership-ground-offset" />`GROUND_OFFSET` | `0.06` | Marker lift off Z (0–2). |
| <a id="config-dealership-av-lift" />`AV_LIFT` | `1.2` | Metres above the dealer an AV is handed over (0–10). |
| <a id="config-dealership-scan-ms" />`SCAN_MS` | `500` | Client marker and zone scan interval. |
| <a id="config-dealership-poll-ms" />`POLL_MS` | `15000` | How often the client re-asks for dealers. |
| <a id="config-dealership-request-window-ms" />`REQUEST_WINDOW_MS` | `10000` | Per-player request window. |
| <a id="config-dealership-requests-per-window" />`REQUESTS_PER_WINDOW` | `6` | Requests allowed per window (buy, offer, decide). |
| <a id="config-dealership-cooldown-ms" />`COOLDOWN_MS` | `3000` | Minimum gap between two purchases. |
| <a id="config-dealership-key" />`KEY` | `{ ID = 'opx.dealership.use', NAME = 'dealership.key.use', DEFAULT = 'E' }` | Key mapping, separate from the garages key. `DEFAULT = false` registers none. |
| <a id="config-dealership-menu" />`MENU` | `{ ANCHOR = 'center', WIDTH = 708, HEIGHT = 708, MAX_HEIGHT_VH = 88, VISIBLE_ROWS = 15 }` | List panel size; clamped by the menu module. |
| <a id="config-dealership-commands" />`COMMANDS` | `{ list = 'opx.dealership.list', stock = 'opx.dealership.stock', buy = 'opx.dealership.buy' }` | Command names. |
| <a id="config-dealership-preview" />`PREVIEW` | `{ ENABLED = true, LIMIT = 12, LOCKED = true, LIFT = 0.1, POINTS = {} }` | Showroom cars. `LIMIT` per dealer; `LIFT` metres added to Z (0–2). `POINTS` below. |
| <a id="config-dealership-zone-radius" />`ZONE_RADIUS` | `30.0` | Flat metres around a dealer where the "sell a vehicle" row appears. Must be ≥ `USE_RADIUS`. |
| <a id="config-dealership-offer-timeout-ms" />`OFFER_TIMEOUT_MS` | `30000` | How long a buyer has to answer an offer. |
| <a id="config-dealership-seller-cut-percent" />`SELLER_CUT_PERCENT` | `10` | Seller's commission, 0–100, rounded down. The rest goes to the company. |
| <a id="config-dealership-company" />`COMPANY` | `{ JOBS = true, GANGS = true, EXCLUDED = { unemployed = true, none = true } }` | Which groups have a company account. Both off: nobody can sell to another player. |
| <a id="config-dealership-stock" />`STOCK` | 30 rows in classes Street, Sport, Utility, Bikes, Air | What is sold. Below. |
| <a id="config-dealership-spots" />`SPOTS` | `garage1` (one `garage` dealer) | Every dealer. Below. |

```lua title="config/dealership.lua — one STOCK row"
{ KEY = 'hella', LABEL = 'Archer Hella', CLASS = 'Street',
  RECORD = 'Vehicle.v_standard2_archer_hella_player', PRICE = 29000 },
```

- `KEY`: 1–48 characters, unique. `LABEL`: 1–64. `CLASS`: 1–32.
- `RECORD`: a TweakDB record (≤ 256). Whether the row is an AV comes from `RECORD` and `AV_PREFIXES` in `config/shared.lua`.
- `PRICE`: a whole number above zero.
- A bad row is skipped with a boot warning.

```lua title="config/dealership.lua — a dealer and a preview point"
SPOTS = {
    watson_autos = { LABEL = 'WATSON AUTOS', KIND = 'garage',
        X = -1771.79, Y = -77.30, Z = 7.53, HEADING = 90.0, BUCKET = 0 },
},
PREVIEW = { POINTS = {
    show_hella_1 = { X = -1536.1, Y = -205.9, Z = 7.86, HEADING = 52.0,
        BUCKET = 0, DEALER = 'watson_autos', ENTRY = 'hella' },
} },
```

- Both follow the shared [spot rules](/docs/opx_infinity/core/lib#spots): key 1–48 characters; `X`, `Y`, `Z` finite within ±1,000,000; `HEADING` finite (default `0`); `BUCKET` whole ≥ 0 (default `0`); `LABEL` defaults to the key. Only X and Y are measured.
- A dealer needs `KIND` `garage` or `avpad`.
- A preview needs `DEALER` (a key in `SPOTS`) and `ENTRY` (a key in `STOCK`).
- To capture a position and heading, stand there facing the right way and run `/opx.admin.self.pos`.

## Refusal codes [#codes]

Each code is also a locale key. Codes from [`vehicles.Register`](/docs/opx_infinity/vehicles#codes) (e.g. `vehicle.limit`) pass through after a refund.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `dealership.noVehicles` | No `vehicles` contract. |
| `dealership.noCharacter` | No character loaded (or it changed before the answer). |
| `dealership.noPosition` | Position could not be read. |
| `dealership.noSuchSpot` | No dealer underfoot or by that key. |
| `dealership.wrongBucket` | The dealer is in another routing bucket. |
| `dealership.tooFar` | Further than `USE_RADIUS`. |
| `dealership.noSuchEntry` | No stock row by that key (or its price changed since the offer). |
| `dealership.notSold` | This dealer's kind does not sell that row. |
| `dealership.noCurrency` | `CURRENCY` is not a money type. |
| `dealership.cannotAfford` | Not enough money. |
| `dealership.noSuchGarage` | The chosen garage (counter or offer) is not one of this kind in the buyer's bucket. |
| `dealership.paymentFailed` | The charge was refused (or the character module's own code). |
| `dealership.registerFailed` | `vehicles.Register` failed unexpectedly; the price was refunded. |
| `dealership.noCompany` | The seller has no job or gang with an account. |
| `dealership.noSuchBuyer` | Bad buyer id, or the seller targeted themself. |
| `dealership.notInZone` / `dealership.buyerNotInZone` | Seller or buyer outside `ZONE_RADIUS`. `Offer` answers `buyerNotInZone` for **any** id outside the showroom, connected or not, before reading the buyer's character. |
| `dealership.buyerCannotAfford` | No longer answered. Since PR #93 the offer always goes out; on accept the **buyer** reads `dealership.cannotAfford` and the seller only `dealership.paymentFailed`, so offers at rising prices reveal nothing about a stranger's balance. |
| `dealership.noOffer` | No offer waiting, or the token does not match. |
| `dealership.offerDeclined` / `dealership.offerExpired` | The buyer said no, or did not answer in time. |
| `dealership.sellerGone` / `dealership.buyerGone` | The other side left or walked out of the zone. |
| `dealership.sellerNoCompany` | At accept, the seller no longer sells for that company. |
| `dealership.handOverBlocked` | Not a refusal: every hand-over spot was taken, the car is in its garage. |
| `dealership.nothingForSale` / `dealership.noList` | Client only: empty stock, or the menu could not open. |
| `error.noPermission` | Client only: another surface holds the keyboard. |
| `error.tooFast`, `error.badRequest` | Cooldown or request window; malformed request. |
