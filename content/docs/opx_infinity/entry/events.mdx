## opx:on:entry:state [#opx-on-entry-state]

Client, local. Which screen of the join is up.

```lua
AddEventHandler('opx:on:entry:state', function(state) end)
```

- state: `table` — `{ open, phase }`
  - `phase`: `'creator'`, `'name'`, `'wardrobe'` (a fitting room is still owed) or `'idle'`.
  - `open`: `false` only for `'idle'`.

## Listened to

- `opx:on:character:loaded`, `changed` and `unloaded`.
- The appearance module's `opx:on:appearance:decision`.
- Refusals on `opx:net:runtime:notify` whose `operation` is `name`.
