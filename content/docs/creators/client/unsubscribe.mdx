---
title: Unsubscribe
description: Stops forwarding an OPX client event to your resource.
---

<a id="export-client-unsubscribe" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:Unsubscribe(event)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- event: `string`

**Returns**
- `boolean` — `true` if you were subscribed

**Errors**
- `export.notSubscribable`, `export.badArgument`, `export.callerDenied`
