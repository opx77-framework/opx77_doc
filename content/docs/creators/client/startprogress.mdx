---
title: StartProgress
description: Runs an OPX progress bar for your resource on the local player.
---

<a id="export-client-startprogress" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:StartProgress(spec)
```

Sync. The end reaches your [reply export](/docs/creators/client#replies) as `opx:on:progress:done` with `{ owner, label, ending, finished }`; only `finished = true` means the action completed. See the [progress module](/docs/opx_infinity/progress).

**Parameters**
- spec: `table`
  - label: `string`
  - durationMs: `integer`
  - cancelable?: `boolean` — the player may cancel with the progress [`CANCEL_KEY`](/docs/opx_infinity/progress#config-progress-cancel-key)
  - animation?: `{ name, variant? }`
  - reply?: `string`

**Returns**
- `{ owner, durationMs }`

**Errors**
- `progress_busy` (one bar at a time for the whole player), `export.callerDenied`, `export.badArgument`, `error.unavailable` (module not running), and the module's codes

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:StartProgress({ label = 'Packing', durationMs = 4000, cancelable = true })

AddEventHandler('opx:on:progress:done', function(p)
	if p.finished then TriggerServerEvent('my_shop:packed') end
end)
```
