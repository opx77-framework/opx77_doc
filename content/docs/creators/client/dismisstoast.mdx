---
title: DismissToast
description: Removes a toast your resource showed.
---

<a id="export-client-dismisstoast" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:DismissToast(id)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- id: `string` — the `id` you gave [`ShowToast`](/docs/creators/client/showtoast)

**Returns**
- `true`

**Errors**
- `export.badArgument`, `export.callerDenied`

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:DismissToast('order')
```
