---
title: StopProgress
description: Stops the progress bar your resource started.
---

<a id="export-client-stopprogress" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:StopProgress()
```

Sync. You get `opx:on:progress:done` with `ending = 'stopped'`.

**Returns**
- as the progress module's [`Stop`](/docs/opx_infinity/progress/client#client-progress-stop)

**Errors**
- `export.callerDenied`, `error.unavailable`, the module's codes
