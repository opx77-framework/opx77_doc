---
title: UpdateMenu
description: Patches a menu your resource opened.
---

<a id="export-client-updatemenu" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:UpdateMenu(handle, spec)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- handle: `string` — from `OpenMenu`
- spec: `table` — fields as the menu's `Update`

**Returns**
- `true`

**Errors**
- `stale_handle` (not one of yours), `export.callerDenied`, `export.badArgument`, `error.unavailable` (module not running), and the menu's codes

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:UpdateMenu(handle, { title = 'NIGHT MARKET — SOLD OUT' })
```
