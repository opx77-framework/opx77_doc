---
title: RemoveTarget
description: Removes one or several of your eye rows.
---

<a id="export-client-removetarget" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:RemoveTarget(tokens)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- tokens: `string | string[]`

- A list is removed whole or not at all. Another resource's token is refused.

**Returns**
- `true`

**Errors**
- `export.ownerTaken`, `export.badArgument`, target codes

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:RemoveTarget(token)
```
