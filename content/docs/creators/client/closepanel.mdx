---
title: ClosePanel
description: Closes your panel.
---

<a id="export-client-closepanel" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:ClosePanel(handle)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- handle: `integer`

- Your reply export hears `opx:on:panel:action` with `action = 'close'`.

**Returns**
- `true`

**Errors**
- `stale_handle`

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:ClosePanel(handle)
```
