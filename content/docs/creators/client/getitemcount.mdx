---
title: GetItemCount
description: Counts an item in the local player's bag.
---

<a id="export-client-getitemcount" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:GetItemCount(item)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- item: `string`

- From the bag the client was last sent.

**Returns**
- `integer`

**Errors**
- `export.badArgument`

```lua title="Example"
local res = exports.opx_infinity:GetItemCount('lockpick')
```
