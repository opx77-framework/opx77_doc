---
title: ShowToast
description: Shows an OPX toast on the local player's screen.
---

<a id="export-client-showtoast" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:ShowToast(definition)
```

Sync.

**Parameters**
- definition: `table`
  - message: `string` — cut to 240 characters
  - kind?: `string` — e.g. `'success'`
  - title?: `string` — cut to 64 characters
  - icon?: `string` — a [glyph name](/docs/opx_infinity/core/client#opx-toast-show)
  - durationMs?: `integer`
  - id?: `string` — letters, digits, `_ - .`, max 48. Kept in your own namespace: showing the same `id` again replaces your toast.

**Returns**
- the toast id

**Errors**
- `export.badArgument`, `export.callerDenied`, or the toast's own refusal

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:ShowToast({ id = 'order', message = 'Order ready', kind = 'success' })
```
