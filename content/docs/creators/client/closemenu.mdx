---
title: CloseMenu
description: Closes a menu your resource opened.
---

<a id="export-client-closemenu" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:CloseMenu(handle)
```

Sync. The close reaches your reply export with `reason = 'caller'`.

**Parameters**
- handle: `string`

**Returns**
- `true`

**Errors**
- `stale_handle` (not one of yours), `export.callerDenied`, `export.badArgument`, `error.unavailable` (module not running)
