---
title: GetGang
description: A copy of a player's primary gang.
---

<a id="export-server-getgang" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:GetGang(source)
```

Scope `read` · sync.

**Parameters**
- source: `number`

**Returns**
- the primary gang table, as the [Player object](/docs/opx_infinity/character/player#player) holds it

**Errors**
- `export.badArgument`, `error.notLoggedIn`
