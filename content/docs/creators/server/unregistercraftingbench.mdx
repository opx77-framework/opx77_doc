---
title: UnregisterCraftingBench
description: Removes one of your crafting benches.
---

<a id="export-server-unregistercraftingbench" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:UnregisterCraftingBench(key)
```

Scope `write` · sync.

**Parameters**
- key: `string` — your key, as you registered it

- Only `<your resource>:<key>`: another resource's bench is never named.

**Returns**
- `boolean` — whether a bench went

**Errors**
- `not_owner`, `export.badArgument`, `export.callerDenied`

```lua title="Example"
exports.opx_infinity:UnregisterCraftingBench('bar')
```
