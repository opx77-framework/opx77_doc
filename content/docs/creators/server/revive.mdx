---
title: Revive
description: Gets a downed player up where the body lies.
---

<a id="export-server-revive" />

```lua
local res = exports.opx_infinity:Revive(source, reason)
```

Scope `write` · sync — memory only, it answers at once.

**Parameters**
- source: `number`
- reason?: `string` — up to 256 characters, cut to 64 in the log

- Goes through the [downed module](/docs/opx_infinity/downed)'s own revive.
- Your resource must be allowed by the module's `REVIVERS` (everyone by default), or it answers `caller_denied`.
- The audit line names your resource and `reason`.

**Returns**
- `true`

**Errors**
- `not_down`, `not_incarnated`, `gate_closed`, `caller_denied`, `export.badArgument`, `error.unavailable`

```lua title="Example"
local res = exports.opx_infinity:Revive(patientId, 'medkit')
if not res.ok then print(res.error) end
```
