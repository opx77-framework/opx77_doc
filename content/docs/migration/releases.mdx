## opx_infinity — PRs #58–#87 (2026-10-02 to 2026-10-04) [#infinity-58-87]

Merged into `main`; the manifest version is still `0.1.2`. One table per group of PRs, newest first. Before updating a running server, read [Upgrading from PR #57](#upgrade-58-87).

### Animations with everyone (PR #87) [#infinity-87]

| Change | Pages |
|---|---|
| The emote picker (F3, `/e`) offers **every platform animation**: 104 on op77.123, in 12 families (Sitting, Dance and music, Work, On the move…). Animations a newer build adds appear at the next start. One-shot gestures (wave, shrug…) play once for their real length; the picker shows 16 per page and has a search. | [animations](/docs/opx_infinity/animations), [catalogue](/docs/opx_infinity/animations/catalogue) |
| **Emotes with a nearby player**: carry or be carried, escort, hand something over, look after someone, two shortcuts, or any two animations, one each. From the picker's *With a nearby player* or `/e with <move\|name> [name]`. | [With a nearby player](/docs/opx_infinity/animations#duo) |
| The nearest player within 3 m is invited and answers from a menu or with `/e accept` / `/e decline`; an invitation expires after 15 s, the distance is checked again on accept, and the stop key ends the pair for both. Invitations count against `RATE_LIMIT`. | [animations](/docs/opx_infinity/animations#duo) |
| New config `PLATFORM` and `SHARED`; `DISABLED` also takes platform profile ids and paired-move ids. New events `opx:net:animations:duo`, `invite`, `uninvite`, `reply`, `notice`; `offer` now arrives in parts. | [Configuration](/docs/opx_infinity/animations#configuration), [Events](/docs/opx_infinity/animations/events) |

### Door locks (PRs #70, #80, #86) [#infinity-doorlock]

| Change | Pages |
|---|---|
| New module **doorlock**, a port of ox_doorlock: doors open by job, gang, grade, key item, character or code. Press E at the door or use the eye's *Lock door* / *Unlock door*. Doors can relock themselves and play a sound. | [doorlock](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock) |
| A pickable door takes a lockpick: one progress bar per step, the rolls are made by the server. The lockpick must still be in the bag, and the player not down, when the attempt ends. | [At a door](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock#player) |
| Staff panel: `/opx.doorlock [closest]`, the F9 *World → Manage door locks* row or the eye's *Manage door*. A searchable, paged table of doors; settings in tabs (General, Characters, Groups, Items, Lockpick, Sound); *Pick in world*, *Give me a key*, copy and apply settings. | [Staff panel](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock#staff-panel) |
| Admins are asked for the key or code like anyone else. The bypass is the plain right `opx.doorlock.bypass` and only works with `STAFF_BYPASS = true` (off by default). Staff holding `command.opx.doorlock.save` see the door's code in the panel. | [Who may turn a door](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock#access) |
| Two backends: `networked` (the platform's `open77_doors`, refused to everyone server-side) and `local` (each client holds its own copy — weaker). | [Backends](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock#backends) |
| Doors saved by the first version carry over at boot; keys already cut keep opening their door. | [Migration](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock#migration) |
| Creator exports `GetDoor`, `GetDoorFromName`, `GetAllDoors`, `SetDoorState`, `SetDoorLocked`, `CreateDoor`, `EditDoor`, `RemoveDoor`, and the public server event `opx:on:doorlock:changed`. | [Server exports → Doors](/docs/creators/server#doors), [Events](/docs/creators/events#opx-on-doorlock-changed) |

## opx_infinity 0.1.2 (September 2026) [#infinity-0-1-2]

The first release of the single resource. It replaces twenty-one `opx77_*` resources (sixteen of them were documented on this site).

| Change | Read |
|---|---|
| One resource, `opx_infinity`, with a core runtime and modules under `modules/<id>/`. | [Overview](/docs/opx_infinity) |
| Modules talk through in-process contracts (`OPX.Api`); **no Open77 exports** are published. | [Modules and contracts](/docs/opx_infinity/concepts/modules-and-contracts) |
| Event names moved to `opx:net:`, `opx:on:`, `opx:in:`. | [Events and channels](/docs/opx_infinity/concepts/events) |
| Commands moved from `opx77.*` to `opx.*`, with optional short aliases. | [Core configuration](/docs/opx_infinity/core/config#config-server-command-aliases) |
| One WebUI page for every screen. | [The WebUI page](/docs/opx_infinity/concepts/webui) |
| Garages, dealers and showroom cars are written in config; legacy tables are adopted at boot. | [garages](/docs/opx_infinity/garages), [dealership](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership) |
| Modules that had no page on the old site: spawn, entry, theme, diagnostics, downed, target, progress, panel, calls, blips, shops, crafting, gunsmith, hauling, teleports, garages, dealership, clothing, vehicles. | [Modules](/docs/opx_infinity) |
