## opx_infinity — PRs #96–#101 (2026-10-05) [#infinity-96-101]

Merged into `main` and shipped in the manifest version `0.2.0` (PR #123). One table per theme. Before updating a running server or a resource that calls OPX, read [Upgrading from PR #95](#upgrade-96-101). (#98, a WebUI polish pass, is not covered here.)

### Four rules the server keeps (PR #101) [#infinity-101]

| What players see now | Before | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger and thirst fall on the **server's** clock and go up only by eating or drinking, staff ([`/opx.needs.set`](/docs/opx_infinity/needs#opx-needs-set)) or a server export. A modified client can no longer stay fed. | The client decayed its own needs and pushed them; the server stored whatever came. | [needs](/docs/opx_infinity/needs) |
| Global chat needs a loaded character: on the character screen a line answers *you speak once your character is in the city*. Staff ([`STAFF_PERMISSION`](/docs/opx_infinity/chat#config-chat-staff-permission)) still can. | Any admitted connection spoke to everyone. | [chat](/docs/opx_infinity/chat) |
| An outfit share code is typed **inside a shop's fitting room** and the pieces are billed at that shop's prices on Save. | A code dressed you for free, anywhere. | [shops](/docs/opx_infinity/shops#outfits) |
| A give asks the receiver: an Accept / Refuse card with the item, the count and where the giver stands, never a name. 15 s to answer. | The stack moved on the giver's press alone. | [Give and drop](/docs/opx_infinity/inventory#give-drop) |

### Player flows (PR #96) [#infinity-96]

| Change | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Bandage, Bounce Back and MaxDoc heal** 15 %, 40 %, 75 % of maximum health ([`HEALING`](/docs/opx_infinity/inventory#config-inventory-healing)); refused at full health and while down; nothing is used up unless the heal goes through. | [Medical items](/docs/opx_infinity/inventory#healing) |
| Withdrawing notes shows one toast; notes handed over are told to both players as money. Drawing a weapon closes the inventory. The give list refreshes every time a slot card opens. | [inventory](/docs/opx_infinity/inventory#give-drop) |
| A shop uniform is billed **on Save**, not when picked; Cancel costs nothing. The fitting room shows the bill before Save (*On Save: 1,050 €$*). A long browse no longer ends in "Your outfit was not saved." (kept alive up to 30 min). | [shops](/docs/opx_infinity/shops#billing) |
| Contacts: DELETE on each row (two presses). A full list (64) refuses new contacts instead of dropping the oldest; offline contacts show greyed. *X is calling you.* / *Calling X...* toasts are gone. | [calls](/docs/opx_infinity/calls) |
| Garage toasts name the car (*Brought out Archer Hella · AA111AA.*, *Recalled … here.*). On foot, the garage door opens the list (*Open your garage*). | [garages](/docs/opx_infinity/garages) |

| The spawn menu waits for a slow name form or fitting room (up to 30 min) instead of placing the player under it. | [spawn](/docs/opx_infinity/spawn#flow) |

### Edge cases and robustness (PRs #97, #100) [#infinity-97-100]

| Change | Pages |
|---|---|
| Two staff door writes at once run one after the other; a wait over 5 s answers `doorlock.error.busy`. A numeric door code of 2^63 or more is refused. | [doorlock](/docs/opx_infinity/doorlock#codes) |
| Two players fetching at one garage get two exits (a bay is promised while the car spawns). | [garages](/docs/opx_infinity/garages) |
| Crafting and hauling refunds and pay go to the **character**, not the connection: leaving or switching character mid-action loses nothing and pays nobody else. | [crafting](/docs/opx_infinity/crafting#benches), [hauling](/docs/opx_infinity/hauling) |
| Money balances stop at ±(2^53−1) (`money.tooLarge`); a staff rename can no longer break saves (name column cut to 64). | [character](/docs/opx_infinity/character#codes) |
| Names refuse invisible and direction-changing characters (`character.nameInvisible`); accents and other alphabets still pass. | [character](/docs/opx_infinity/character#configuration) |
| Broken UTF-8 in chat, contact names and labels is mended to `?` instead of breaking saves. | [OPX.Text.Clean](/docs/opx_infinity/core/lib#opx-text-clean) |
| A chat config missing `MAX_LENGTH` / `RATE_MS` / `READY_MS` takes the defaults instead of silencing chat. | [chat](/docs/opx_infinity/chat#configuration) |
| Dealership deposits and crafting placements are idempotent: a lost answer never pays twice or refunds an order that exists. | [dealership](/docs/opx_infinity/dealership), [crafting](/docs/opx_infinity/crafting#benches) |
| Stashes opened by players are put away when idle; a lift finishes its trip when a rider leaves; two vehicle registrations for one character cannot pass the cap (`vehicle.registering`). | [inventory](/docs/opx_infinity/inventory#give-drop), [elevators](/docs/opx_infinity/elevators), [vehicles](/docs/opx_infinity/vehicles#codes) |

## opx_infinity 0.1.2 (September 2026) [#infinity-0-1-2]

The first release of the single resource. It replaces twenty-one `opx77_*` resources (sixteen of them were documented on this site).

| Change | Read |
|---|---|
| One resource, `opx_infinity`, with a core runtime and modules under `modules/<id>/`. | [Overview](/docs/opx_infinity) |
