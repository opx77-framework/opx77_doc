title: Lib.Anim
description: Play an emote on the local player or an animation on another body.
---

No permission declared by the module.

## Lib.Anim.Self [#lib-anim-self]

Plays an emote on the local player.

```lua
Lib.Anim.Self(animation, thirdPerson)
```

**Parameters**
- animation: `string`
- thirdPerson?: `boolean` — pull the camera out for the clip

**Returns**
- `Result` — error `invalid_animation`

```lua title="Example"
Lib.Anim.Self('wave', true)
```

## Lib.Anim.On [#lib-anim-on]

Plays an animation on another body (never the local player).

```lua
Lib.Anim.On(entity, animation)
```

**Parameters**
- entity: `number`
- animation: `string`

**Returns**
- `Result` — errors `invalid_entity`, `is_local_player`, `invalid_animation`
